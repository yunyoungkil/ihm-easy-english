05

Principles

Craft Bar · Squint · Silence · Restraint
Do · squint

One thing holds the light.

One element dominates at 3–6× its nearest neighbour. The 3×2 card grid reads as a single mass, not six equal cards; the giant action reads as a moon.

Do · silence

Warm-gray silence is ambient light.

Sparse archetypes read 55–75% empty. Ledger and Catalog are the two named density exceptions — every other frame breathes.

Do · restraint

One voltage per frame.

Exactly one lavender focus glow, one red-dot signal, one giant focal artifact. If two things want to glow, demote one to plain surface.

Do · anchor

Lean centred.

Cover, claim, focal-artifact and closer all centre their focal element. Left anchor is reserved for ledger and catalog. Vary anchor across the sequence.

Don't · shadow

No sharp drop shadows.

Wide-blur or nothing. 0 3px 5px #999-class shadows break the ambient lighting instantly.

Don't · saturation

No high-saturation colour.

If a colour reads like Material or iOS default blue, it is wrong. Every accent stays inside the muted family; no black outlines, ever.

Don't · slideshow

No headline top-left with an image parked in the right.

That is the deck tell. Anchor varies every 2 frames maximum; the eyebrow appears on a minority of frames, not all.

Don't · density

Never more than 2–3 things on a frame.

Two things to say → two frames, not a denser one. Sparse archetypes read empty by design; the density exceptions are named (ledger, catalog).
