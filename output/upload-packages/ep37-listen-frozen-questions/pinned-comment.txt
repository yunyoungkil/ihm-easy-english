질문 서른 개와 대답 (영어 · 우리말 뜻 · 들을 곳)

[1장 길·지하철]
1. How do I get to Seoul Station? — 서울역 어떻게 가요? / Take Line 1. (크게 들리는 두 군데: get, Seoul Station)
2. Which line goes to Gangnam? — 강남 가려면 몇 호선 타요? / The green one, Line 2. (line, Gangnam)
3. Is this the right way to Myeongdong? — 명동 가는 길, 이쪽 맞아요? / Yes, go straight. (right way, Myeongdong)
4. Where can I buy a ticket? — 표는 어디서 사요? / Over there, at the machine. (can이 '큰'처럼 작아짐, can't는 안 작아짐)
5. Does this train go to the airport? — 이 열차 공항 가요? / Yes, it does. (train, airport)
6. How long does it take to get there? — 거기까지 얼마나 걸려요? / About twenty minutes. (how long, take)

[2장 우리 가게]
7. How much is it? — 얼마예요? / It's fifteen thousand won. (much is it 한 덩어리)
8. Can I pay by card? — 카드로 계산해도 돼요? / Sure, go ahead. (Can I → 캐나이)
9. Is it spicy? — 매워요? / Just a little. (Is it → 이짓)
10. Do you have this in black? — 이걸로 검은색 있어요? / Yes, right here. (this in → 디씬)
11. Can I take a look? — 좀 봐도 돼요? / Of course. (붙는 곳 두 군데: 캐나이, 테이커)
12. Is this on sale? — 이거 할인해요? / Yes, thirty percent off. (this on → 디쏜)

[3장 처음 만난 사람]
13. Did you just move in? — 막 이사 오셨어요? / Yeah, last week. (Did you → 디쥬)
14. Would you like some coffee? — 커피 드실래요? / Yes, please. (Would you → 우쥬)
15. Is that your dog? — 그 강아지 키우시는 거예요? / Yes, she's very friendly. (that your → 대츄어 또는 트가 짧게 끊김)
16. Could you help me with this? — 이것 좀 도와주실래요? / No problem. (Could you → 쿠쥬)
17. Can I get you anything? — 뭐 좀 갖다 드릴까요? / Just water, thanks. (get you → 게츄 또는 트가 짧게 끊김)
18. Where did you get that? — 그거 어디서 샀어요? / At the market nearby. (did you → 디쥬)

[4장 여행지]
19. What are you looking for? — 뭐 찾으세요? / Just looking, thanks. (What are you → 워러유)
20. Should I put it in a bag? — 봉투에 넣어 드릴까요? / No, thanks. (put it in a → 푸리리너)
21. Are you waiting for a taxi? — 택시 기다리세요? / Yes, I am. (waiting → 웨이링)
22. Is that it? — 이게 다예요? / Yes, that's all. (that it → 대릿, 뜻 '이게 다예요?')
23. What about this one? — 이건 어떠세요? / That looks good. (What about → 워러바웃)
24. Is it a little too big? — 좀 큰가요? / Maybe a little. (it a little → 이지러 리를)

[5장 친한 사이]
25. Want some? — 좀 먹을래? / Sure, thanks. (Do you 빠짐, 끝 올림)
26. Ready? — 준비됐어? / Almost. (Are you 빠짐)
27. Got a minute? — 잠깐 시간 돼? / Yeah, what's up? (Have you 빠짐 + got a 구름)
28. Need a hand? — 도와줄까? / No, I'm good. (Do you 빠짐 + need a 붙음 + 뜻을 돌려 말함)
29. Hungry? — 배고파? / Yeah, starving. (Are you 빠짐)
30. Seen this? — 이거 봤어? / No, what is it? (Have you 빠짐)
