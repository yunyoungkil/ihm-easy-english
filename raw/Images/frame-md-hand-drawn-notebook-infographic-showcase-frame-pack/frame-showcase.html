06 Principles

70% clean information design + 30% hand-drawn imperfection. Lean centered by default. Ration doodles. One marker voltage per plate — the ledger is the one named density exception.

Do
  • Lean centered — cover, editorial, focal-artifact, brand-signature, closer.
  • One idea per plate; two things → two plates.
  • Let ivory paper carry the silence (55–75% empty).
  • Ration doodles: header 6–8, middle 2–4, bottom 8–12.
  • Keep marker rounded asymmetric — 12/8/11/7.
  • Full-bleed one atom every 3–4 plates.
Don't
  • No dark backgrounds, no glassmorphism, no glossy 3D.
  • No new hexes, radii, or fonts.
  • No gradient except strength-bar + ground radial.
  • No doodle over a headline / badge / marker (≥2cqw keep-out).
  • No top-left eyebrow on every plate.
  • No web chrome as a treatment.
Voltage
  • Pink highlight is only the subtitle voice.
  • Yellow / Blue / Orange / Green mark section titles.
  • Features header uses features-pink.
  • Strength bar is the one gradient; never repeat it elsewhere.
  • Badge color ↔ section-head-marked color must match.
Ink
  • All outlines #171719, 2.2–3px, round caps & joins.
  • ±1–2° rotation and ±2px baseline shimmer on long outlines.
  • Right notebook corners far rounder than the left (spiral edge is nearly square).
  • Every card carries the lavender offset shadow — zero blur.
